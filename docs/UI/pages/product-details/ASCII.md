# Layout Principles

* Product information is prioritised above secondary content.
* Pricing and availability remain visible without scrolling on desktop.
* Quantity selection and purchase actions are grouped together.
* Product specifications are displayed separately from the description.
* Supplier information is presented as a dedicated information card.
* Related products encourage continued browsing without distracting from the current product.
* The layout adapts consistently across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices while preserving the purchasing workflow.
